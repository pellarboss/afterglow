# Code of Conduct

Afterglow follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language aren't acceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainer privately through their GitHub profile, or use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue. Reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break this code, and may block people who repeatedly do so.
